# Hi, I'm Ambra Dondi 👋

**Image Analysis Scientist & Technologist** building computational pipelines that extract quantitative insights from complex 3D imaging data. With a PhD in Systems Medicine and over 10 years developing automated analysis workflows, I specialize in deep learning segmentation, morphological analysis, and multi-dimensional signal quantification. My work translates across domains—from biomedical research to materials science, geospatial analysis, and industrial quality control.
